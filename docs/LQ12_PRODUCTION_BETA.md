# LQ12 production validation and controlled beta

The private beta workspace records verified-account observations for phone, tablet and desktop across Find, Qualify, Move, Alerts, Career tools and the full journey.

Launch gates: 10–20 unique participants; at least 80% full-journey completion; at least 90% without technical help; zero unresolved critical issues; phone and desktop represented.

Do not include OTPs, session tokens, admin keys, passport numbers, financial documents, résumé files or raw authority correspondence in feedback. Payment, automatic application submission and external notification delivery remain disabled.
