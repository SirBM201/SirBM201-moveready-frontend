# LQ17 — Evidence-backed Outcome Learning Loop

## V1 acceptance contract

- Distinguish confirmed lifecycle outcomes from unknown post-submission responses.
- Show evidence coverage and require a minimum sample before describing observed patterns.
- Surface the recorded application funnel, observed segment leaders and evidence-safe next actions.
- Use private user-confirmed history only; never infer employer intent, sponsorship, discrimination, eligibility or future success.
- Keep learning advisory and bounded; it must not submit applications or silently change vacancy qualification.

## Production acceptance

Open Intelligence → Outcomes. Confirm recorded applications, confirmed outcomes, unknown responses and evidence coverage match the execution portfolio. With fewer than three applications, the page must show insufficient sample. Update one lifecycle with employer evidence and confirm the dashboard changes while retaining the descriptive-only warning.
