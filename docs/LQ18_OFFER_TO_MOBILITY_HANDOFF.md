# LQ18 — Offer-to-Mobility Handoff

## V1 acceptance contract

- Open mobility planning only from a user-recorded offer or hire state supported by employer evidence.
- Preserve destination, work-authorization, sponsorship, relocation-support and start-date unknowns as explicit gaps.
- Do not treat an employment offer as immigration approval, work authorization or sponsorship evidence.
- Link confirmed offers to route verification, evidence preparation, financial readiness and journey planning.
- Never submit an authority application, contact an employer, book travel or buy a service automatically.

## Production acceptance

Record an offer lifecycle transition with employer evidence. Confirm Execution step 6 appears, unconfirmed work-route or sponsorship evidence remains blocking, and each gap opens the correct MoveReady workspace. After evidence is confirmed, verify that mobility planning becomes available without triggering any external action.
