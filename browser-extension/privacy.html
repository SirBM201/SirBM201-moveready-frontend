MoveReady Safe Application Assistant — privacy

The extension runs only on the launch support list: Greenhouse, Lever, Workday and SmartRecruiters vacancy pages. It reads visible job metadata for preview and reads form labels only when you press the fill button.

Reusable answers are stored locally in Chrome extension storage. The extension does not collect passwords, OTPs, national identifiers, payment details or employer credentials. It never presses a submit button and never sends recruiter messages.

Captured vacancy data leaves the extension only when you explicitly open MoveReady to review and save it under your signed-in account. Uninstalling the extension removes its local profile data under Chrome’s normal extension-storage behavior.
